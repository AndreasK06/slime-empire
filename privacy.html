Slime Empire
Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

  • 💾Your game is saved on your own device (and, in the iPhone app, in your own iCloud).
  • 🚫No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account and no email address.
  • 👥The game only goes online if you turn on Friends. Then your kingdom name and progress are shared with the people who have your friend code.
  • 🗑️You can delete your online data in the game at any time.

Who makes the game

Slime Empire is made by an independent developer (andreask06 on GitHub). In this policy, "we" means the developer.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, friend list and blocked list are saved on your device. They never leave it unless you choose to: for example when you copy an export code, or share or save a Kingdom card picture.

If you delete the game or clear its website data, this save is deleted too.

iPhone app: iCloud backup. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy of your save in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so it comes back if you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud, not by us, and we cannot see it. You can turn it off in the iPhone Settings under your name → iCloud.

Friends: the only online part

Friends is optional. Nothing is sent until you tap Get my friend code in 👥 Friends. After that:

  • An anonymous sign-in. The game signs in to Google Firebase without any name, email or phone number. Firebase gives it a random ID.
  • Your kingdom profile. It is stored under your friend code (6 random letters and numbers). Anyone who has your code can see it:
    • kingdom name
    • rank
    • level
    • best slime
    • best goo per second
    • ascensions and Starfalls
    • stars, moons, lanterns, islands, dreams
    • Dragon Book and Tiamatra
    • Slimeling form
    • Rift depth
    • puzzle and Daily Dream results
    • when it was last updated
    • the random sign-in ID
  • Your inbox. It holds the friend requests and gifts other players send you: their friend code, kingdom name and when they sent it. Only you can read your inbox.
  • A secret key. This lets your save keep its friend code if you move it to a new device. Nobody can read the key.
  • Reports. If you report a kingdom, we store its friend code and name, your friend code and the time, so we can look at it.

Your kingdom name is the only thing you write that other players can see. Please don't use your real name or anything personal. Names with rude words are hidden as "Slime Kingdom". You can block or report any kingdom: tap it in 👥 Friends, or use 🚫 on a friend request or gift.

Where it is stored

The online data is stored with Google Firebase, which works for us as a data processor. The database is in the European Union, in Finland. To provide the service, Google also handles technical data such as your IP address.

When the game starts, and when you come back to it, it asks GitHub Pages (where the game's website lives) for the current time, so changing the phone's clock can't speed the game up. GitHub receives your IP address, like with any web request. Nothing about you or your game is sent.

The web version of the game also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the same way any website does. The app has its fonts built in.

What we never do

  • We don't show ads or sell or share your data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or any account details.

How long it is kept

  • Your profile stays until you delete it.
  • Friend requests and gifts disappear when you accept, decline or open them.
  • Reports are kept only as long as we need them to deal with the kingdom that was reported.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete my online data, or open 👥 Friends and tap Delete my online data. This deletes your profile, your inbox, your secret key and the anonymous sign-in. Your game progress stays on your device.

Deleting the game from your device does not delete the online data, so use the button first.

Children

Slime Empire is made for everyone and never asks for personal information. Friends is optional, rude names are filtered, and kingdoms can be blocked and reported. A parent can delete a child's online data with the button above.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Deleting can be done right in the game. If you live in the EU or EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority. In Norway, that is Datatilsynet.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the game: open an issue on GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
