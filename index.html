Constellations

Spend Stardust to light stars, connect-the-dots style. Drag a line from a lit star to a glowing neighbour, or tap a glowing star and press Light. Every star helps, and a finished shape gives a big bonus.

Royal perks

The Fuse

Minibosses drop Artifacts. Fuse 3 of the same rarity into 1 of the next. Every Artifact boosts all goo, and fusing always raises the bonus.
